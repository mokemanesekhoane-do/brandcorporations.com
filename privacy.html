Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information Brand Corporations collects through brandcorporations.com, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It covers this website only.

What we collect

We collect information you type into the “Get in touch” form on this site. That is:

  • Name and email address — required, so we can reply to you.
  • Company and phone number — optional.
  • Service, subject and message — what you are enquiring about.

We do not ask for payment details, identity numbers, or any other sensitive information anywhere on this site. There is no account to create and no login.

How your enquiry reaches us

When you submit the form, the details you entered are sent to us so that we can respond. Depending on how your browser is set up, this either opens your own email application with the message ready to send, or transmits it directly to our enquiry inbox.

We use what you send only to answer your enquiry and to carry out any work that follows from it. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it for anyone else’s marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Nothing follows you around after you leave.

Third parties

The site loads its typeface (Poppins) from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches those font files, Google receives your IP address and basic request information, as it would for any file requested from a third-party server. We do not send Google anything you type into the form. Google’s handling of that request is covered by its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Enquiries stay in our email records for as long as they are useful to the working relationship, and are deleted when they are not. If you would like your enquiry deleted sooner, ask us and we will do it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email info@brandcorporations.com and we will respond within a reasonable period. You can also simply not use the form — our phone number and address are on the site if you would rather reach us another way.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
